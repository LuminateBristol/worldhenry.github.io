The Startup Starts

It's 1am. I'm sitting on my sofa at home reflecting on the last 6 months since I finished my PhD and started on the startup journey. In many ways it's been a slight drop in intensity compared to the madness of the last 6 months of writing a thesis. The real challenge this time comes from the unknown, the strategic decisions, the spinning plates and the general questioning, at times, of whether any of this was a good idea.

Capturing the Journey

I'm pretty keen to capture the wild ride that is and I'm sure will continue to be the journey of building a robotics startup. I'm no expert here and fully learning as I go along, so the aim isn't to provide any form of advice or training, but instead share some experiences, both good and bad in the process.

We're 6 months in but really only just getting started. We are unfunded other than a few accelerator grants that have paid my time over the last few months. But robotics is expensive, and we're talking real robotics here, hardware, software and everything in-between.

It's way too late to go into the details right now so I'll save the story so far for next time I have a spare moment to brain dump. Keep building.
