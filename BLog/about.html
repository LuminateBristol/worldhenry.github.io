whoami

I'm Henry - a roboticist and maker based in London. I recently finished a PhD in swarm robotics at the Bristol Robotics Lab, building resilient robot swarms that share knowledge through the cloud.

Now I'm working on a startup, to bring the magic of robotics into the real world with the goal of helping people and planet alike.

I also build things that light up: swarm-inspired art installations, wearable electronics and event lighting. The Build Log (Blog) is where I write up what I'm building, breaking, philosophising and more.
